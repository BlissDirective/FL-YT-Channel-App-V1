# Preliminary Niche Research — Faceless YouTube Starting Points

A starting-place shortlist of **10 niches + channel angles** for faceless,
AI-assisted channels run from this studio. Ranked by the metrics that actually
drive a faceless channel's economics. **Treat numbers as illustrative ranges,
not promises** — RPM swings with geography, season, and audience. Validate any
candidate live with the studio's **Scout** chat and **Run intelligence** before
committing.

---

## The metrics this list is built on

1. **RPM / CPM (revenue per 1,000 views)** — the single biggest lever. Set by
   *advertiser demand* in the topic, not by views. Finance, business, and tech
   command multiples of entertainment. (Configurable per project in Settings →
   the dashboard's estimated revenue uses it.)
2. **Watch time & retention** — YouTube rewards minutes-watched. Story-driven
   and "video essay" formats retain far better than thin list content.
3. **Search + evergreen demand** — topics people actively search and that stay
   relevant for years compound; news-cycle topics decay.
4. **Faceless automatability** — can it be made well from VO + licensed
   footage/stock/charts/screen-recording, with no on-camera talent?
5. **Licensed-source fit** — niches the studio's compliant sources (Pexels,
   Internet Archive, Wikimedia, **NASA/gov**, Pixabay) already cover cheaply.
6. **Policy / monetization safety** — avoid YMYL claims, graphic content, and
   reused/“inauthentic” content that risks demonetization.

> Trade-off to remember: the **highest-RPM** niches (finance, business) are the
> **most competitive**; the **highest-demand** niches (history, science, true
> crime) convert watch-time into volume at a **lower RPM**. A portfolio mixes
> both — which is exactly what this app is built to run.

## The 10 niches

### 1. Personal Finance & Investing — *highest RPM*
- **RPM band:** very high (~$12–$30+). Brokerages, fintech, credit, and
  insurance advertisers bid hard.
- **Why faceless:** charts, stock b-roll, and a calm authoritative VO carry it.
- **Demand/competition:** huge demand, **crowded** — win on a sharper angle.
- **Angles:**
  - *Money psychology* — story-first videos on why we sabotage our finances.
  - *One concept, fully explained* — "how compound interest actually works."
  - *Post-mortems* — bank failures, bubbles, famous bankruptcies.
- **Caution:** YMYL — educate, never give individualized investment advice.

### 2. Business & Side-Hustle Case Studies
- **RPM band:** high (~$10–$25).
- **Why faceless:** brand logos (press assets), data viz, narration.
- **Angles:** company strategy teardowns; side-hustle breakdowns *with real
  numbers*; "how this brand won/lost."
- **Caution:** use press-kit/owned imagery, not scraped IP.

### 3. AI, Software & "How-To" Tech
- **RPM band:** moderate–high (~$8–$20); AI tooling is a surging ad category.
- **Why faceless:** **screen recordings** + VO — among the cheapest to produce.
- **Angles:** "AI tool of the week, actually tested"; practical software
  tutorials; "explain this emerging tech in 8 minutes."
- **Edge:** fastest-moving topic pool — pairs well with the daily intelligence
  run for fresh ideas.

### 4. Science & Space Explainers — *best licensed-source fit*
- **RPM band:** moderate (~$4–$12), but **very high retention** and evergreen.
- **Why faceless:** **NASA/gov + Wikimedia + Archive** footage (already in the
  studio's Source Library) + animation + VO. Near-zero footage cost.
- **Angles:** space/astronomy explainers; "the science of everyday things";
  futurism/emerging-science deep dives.

### 5. History & Geopolitics Video Essays — *faceless sweet spot*
- **RPM band:** moderate (~$4–$12); enormous demand, long watch-time, evergreen.
- **Why faceless:** archival (Archive/Wikimedia, public-domain), maps, narration
  — the classic high-performing faceless format.
- **Angles:** "why X actually happened"; hidden histories; conflict/empire
  explainers; "the story behind [thing you use daily]."

### 6. Health, Longevity & Sleep (evidence-based)
- **RPM band:** high (~$8–$20); wellness/supplement advertisers.
- **Why faceless:** stock + clean animation + measured VO.
- **Angles:** the science of sleep/habits/nutrition; "what the research actually
  says"; longevity protocols explained.
- **Caution:** **strongest YMYL risk** — cite peer-reviewed sources, avoid
  medical/cure claims; the QC agent's policy check matters most here.

### 7. Real Estate & Wealth-Building
- **RPM band:** very high (~$10–$25).
- **Why faceless:** market data, stock property footage, narration.
- **Angles:** local/national market breakdowns; "how someone built a portfolio";
  REIT/housing-policy explainers.

### 8. Self-Improvement, Stoicism & Psychology
- **RPM band:** moderate (~$6–$15); evergreen and highly shareable.
- **Why faceless:** cinematic stock + animation + reflective VO.
- **Angles:** stoicism for modern life; the psychology of habits/discipline;
  "the system that fixed my focus."

### 9. True Crime / Scams & Frauds (compliant)
- **RPM band:** moderate (~$5–$12); **massive binge watch-time**.
- **Why faceless:** narration + archival/stock; no on-camera needed.
- **Angles:** unsolved mysteries; "how this scam worked"; corporate-fraud
  breakdowns (overlaps finance demand at higher RPM).
- **Caution:** keep it non-graphic and respectful — graphic content risks
  demonetization; favor financial/corporate crime for higher RPM + safety.

### 10. Engineering & "How It Works" (cars, infrastructure, gadgets)
- **RPM band:** moderate–high (~$6–$18); automotive/industrial advertisers.
- **Why faceless:** manufacturer **press media** + stock + animation + VO.
- **Angles:** "the engineering behind [hypercar/bridge/chip]"; why things are
  built the way they are; product/industrial history.
- **Caution (and the gaming note):** for any IP-heavy lane — game footage,
  branded product video — use **official press-kit / owned-capture** sources
  only (the studio's policy), never scraped publisher IP.

## Suggested starting portfolio

A balanced first three that hedges RPM against demand and reuses the studio's
free licensed sources:

- **Anchor (RPM):** Personal Finance — money-psychology angle.
- **Volume (demand + retention):** History/Science video essays — runs almost
  entirely on free Archive/NASA/Wikimedia footage.
- **Velocity (fresh ideas + cheap production):** AI/Software how-to — screen
  recordings, and the daily intelligence run keeps the topic queue full.

## Validate before you commit (in-app)

1. **Scout chat** on a draft project: *"tear down the top channels in [niche]"*
   and *"what proven topics am I missing?"* — it pulls real titles + view counts.
2. **Run intelligence** to land scored, repurposable idea cards from the live
   niche so you can judge real demand and angle headroom.
3. Set the project's **niche RPM** in Settings to that niche's band so the
   estimated-revenue figures are realistic from day one.

*Preliminary — a starting hypothesis to test with live data, not a final call.*
