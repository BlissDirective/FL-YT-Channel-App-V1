# GTA 6 Channel — Branding

Naming and visual-identity options for the **GTA 6 news, lore & analysis**
channel. Built for the compliant lane: **original brandable marks**, never
Rockstar's GTA wordmark, the official "VI" lockup, or trailer/key art.

> **Naming principle:** use a distinctive coined brand as the *mark*, with
> "GTA 6 News, Lore & Analysis" as a **descriptor** in the handle/description —
> not "Grand Theft Auto" or the stylized VI as your logo. Verify the YouTube
> `@handle`, domain/socials, and run a trademark sanity check before locking.

## 10 channel names

### Tier A — original & ownable (lowest TM risk, strongest brand)
| Name | Vibe / fit |
|---|---|
| **Neon Dispatch** | News-roundup energy + Miami neon. Clean, brandable. |
| **Six Frames** | "Six" (GTA 6) + frame-by-frame analysis. Perfect for decode videos. |
| **Heist Theory** | "Theory"-channel feel for lore/narrative breakdowns. |
| **Sunset Static** | Moody Miami sunset + "signal/static." Atmospheric, memorable. |
| **Palm & Pavement** | Streets + palms — leans into culture / environmental-storytelling. |

### Tier B — world-evoking (more recognizable, closer to Rockstar's fictional IP — coin it as the brand, "GTA 6" as descriptor, verify first)
| Name | Vibe / fit |
|---|---|
| **Vice Signal** | Vice City + "signal" (intel/news). Strong logo potential. |
| **Leonida Files** | GTA 6's in-game state + investigative "files" = lore deep-dives. |
| **Leonida Lowdown** | Alliterative, friendly news-roundup brand. |
| **The Vice City Almanac** | Reference/almanac framing for the 50-details lore format. |
| **Vice Decoded** | Matches the "trailer secrets decoded" format — clear analysis brand. |

*"Vice," "Vice City," and "Leonida" reference Rockstar's fictional world —
evocative but riskier than the Tier-A coined names.*

## Visual identity

**Vice palette** (set as the project brand kit):

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Hot pink (primary) | `#FF2A6D` | Caption highlights, CTA lower-third, thumbnail accent |
| Night (secondary) | `#0B0B1A` | Intro/end gradient base, thumbnail palette |
| Teal | `#05D9E8` | Secondary accent / data |
| Sunset orange | `#FF7A00` | Warm accent / gradients |
| Off-white | `#F7F3EA` | Text on dark |

Type: heavy geometric sans (**Plus Jakarta Sans Bold**, already in the app).
Thumbnail style: *neon Vice City noir, 80s sunset.*

### Logo / profile-picture concepts
Design for a **circle at 24px** — one bold idea, high contrast, no fine detail.

1. **Neon "6" monogram** — custom bold 6, pink→orange gradient + soft glow on
   near-black. Pairs with *Six Frames / Neon Dispatch*. (Draw your own 6.)
2. **Palm-sunset roundel** — palm silhouette over a teal→magenta sunset circle.
   Pairs with *Sunset Static / Palm & Pavement*.
3. **Waveform → palm** — a neon news waveform whose last bars become a palm
   frond. Pairs with *Vice Signal / Neon Dispatch*.
4. **Frame brackets** — analysis `[ ]` brackets around a tiny neon skyline.
   Pairs with *Six Frames / Vice Decoded*.
5. **Flamingo mark** — minimalist hot-pink flamingo on night-black. Ownable,
   instantly Miami, zero IP overlap.
6. **80s sunset badge** — rounded-square retro sun with a thin "6" notched out.
   Vaporwave/Vice aesthetic, original art.
7. **Lore map-pin** — a pin built from a palm + sun: "exploring the world."
8. **Skyline-under-sun silhouette** — low Miami skyline under a neon sun disc
   (original art — no real car brands, no key art).

### Ready-to-run pairings
- **Neon Dispatch** + **neon-6 monogram** — modern, super legible.
- **Vice Signal** + **waveform→palm** — atmospheric, strong when small.

## Art compliance guardrails
Original artwork only. Avoid Rockstar's GTA logo, the official pink/teal "VI"
lockup, trailer frames, and any "official"-looking styling or wording. A
self-made neon-Miami interpretation is fine and ownable.
