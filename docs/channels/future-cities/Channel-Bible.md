# Channel Bible: Tomorrowscape (app project "Future Cities")

> **Concept:** Spectacular, speculative worldbuilding of future technologies and
> futuristic cities, shown in cinematic detail and grounded in plausible physics
> and engineering. Examples: floating cities, teleportation stations, megacities,
> underground cities powered by energy crystals, deep underwater cities, space and
> Moon cities, walking and rolling cities, and future vehicles. Every episode
> answers **"how could it actually work?"**
> **This is not a news channel.** The old "Blueprint Tomorrow" angle (real
> megaprojects, status checks) is retired. Real projects appear only as
> *grounding references* ("the physics already exists here"), never as the subject.
> **Audience:** 16–45, sci-fi, space, gaming and engineering-curious viewers,
> worldbuilders and solarpunk/cyberpunk aesthetics fans.
> **Research date:** 2026-09-28. **[U]** = unverified (vendor estimate, secondary
> source, or inference). Sources are inline and listed at the end.
> **Companion files:** [Video-Ideas-60.md](./Video-Ideas-60.md) ·
> [Project-Settings.json](./Project-Settings.json)

## 1. Niche and 2026 market trends

### 1.1 What the niche is

| Lane | What it covers | Share | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Speculative cities** | Floating, underwater, underground, walking/rolling, arcology, sky cities | ~45% | The hero visual: whole worlds that cannot be filmed, only imagined |
| **Worlds Beyond** | Moon, Mars, Venus, Titan, O'Neill cylinders, asteroid habitats | ~25% | Space colonisation is an evergreen search theme with high education RPM |
| **Machines of Tomorrow** | Teleport stations, hyperloops, launch loops, eVTOL skyports, floating tunnels | ~20% | Vehicles and motion are ideal for Shorts and loops |
| **Physics Check** | Sci-fi tropes tested ("could this actually stand?") | ~10% | Debate and comment bait; builds the channel's credibility |

**Positioning:** *"Tomorrowscape builds the cities of science fiction in cinematic
detail, and then shows you the physics that could make them real."*

The edge: AI cinematics are everywhere, but most "Future City 4K" uploads are
music-only montages with no narrative or science. We pair a spectacular visual with
a **"how it works" layer** (forces, energy, materials, numbers) and an honest
**Plausibility Meter**. Together these give the channel original insight, which
the inauthentic-content policy (§9) rewards.

### 1.2 Demand drivers in 2026

1. **AI-cinematic "future city" content is viral, but mostly low-effort.**
   - YouTube search is saturated with "Future City 2050/2126 in 4K" AI films
     ([example 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHRqd2RfdBw),
     [example 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6XhrVlUo8aY),
     [example 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blA0p8tJb3I)). TikTok has
     discovery pages dedicated to them
     ([TikTok: AI generated future cities](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/ai-generated-future-cities)).
   - A 2-minute AI tour of a futuristic Tokyo is reported as the most-viewed AI
     video of 2026 [U: single secondary source]
     ([is4.ai](https://is4.ai/blog/our-blog-1/viral-ai-videos-2026-how-they-were-made-204)).
   - The 2026 shift is from one-off clips to **recurring worlds and story
     universes**, which bring audiences back
     ([OpenArt](https://openart.ai/blog/ai-youtube-channel-ideas/)) [U: vendor].
     Our answer is recurring series and a persistent world, "the Tomorrowscape
     atlas", where cities recur across episodes (§4.7).
2. **Solarpunk went mainstream; cyberpunk remains the contrast aesthetic.**
   - Solarpunk "exploded into mainstream consciousness" in June 2026. Pinterest's
     "Terra Futura" trend showed up to 115% search growth for solarpunk keywords
     [U: secondary]
     ([DesignDrop](https://designdrop.ghost.io/solarpunk-aesthetic-eco-futurism-2026/);
     [UVRN](https://www.uvrn.org/research/solarpunk-eco-futurism-botanical-tech-illustration-underserved-pod-niche-2026)).
   - Solarpunk resonates with younger, climate-hopeful audiences who want hopeful
     rather than dystopian futures
     ([UW SMEA](https://smea.uw.edu/currents/solarpunk-a-vision-for-a-sustainable-future/)).
   - Channel stance: **solarpunk is the default optimism**, and cyberpunk is used
     deliberately for density and night-city episodes.
3. **Real science keeps supplying "it could actually work" hooks.**
   - **Moon caves:** radar evidence of an accessible cave conduit below the Mare
     Tranquillitatis pit, published in *Nature Astronomy*, 2024
     ([Nature Astronomy](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-024-02302-y);
     [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2024-07-lunar-lava-tube-cave.html)).
   - **Underwater living:** DEEP's Vanguard habitat was planned for installation
     at the end of April 2026, and the larger Sentinel for 2027. Sentinel is
     planned to host up to 50 people at up to 225 m depth
     ([MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/11/07/1127682/vanguard-deep-subsea-habitat-launch/);
     [DEEP](https://www.deep.com/sentinel)). Vanguard's installation status is [U].
   - **"Energy crystals" have a real analogue:** the carbon-14 diamond battery
     (University of Bristol and UKAEA, Dec 2024) uses a half-life of about
     5,700 years
     ([Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/news/2024/december/diamond-battery-media-release.html);
     [UKAEA](https://www.ukaea.org/case-studies/carbon-14-diamond-battery/)).
   - **Teleportation:** Oxford teleported logical gates between two quantum
     processors (Nature, Feb 2025). This teleports *information*, not matter,
     which makes a strong Physics Check hook
     ([Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/news/2025-02-06-first-distributed-quantum-algorithm-brings-quantum-supercomputers-closer)).
   - **Floating cities:** OCEANIX Busan has stalled on maritime law and mooring
     rights, so the concept is still unbuilt [U: secondary]
     ([Studio Matrx](https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/oceanix-city);
     [BIG](https://big.dk/projects/oceanix-busan-4711)). Japan's N-ARK Dogen City
     proposes 10,000 residents on a tsunami-shielding ring by 2030
     ([New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/architecture/dogen-city-floating-self-sufficient/)).
4. **Classic design studies give us "semi-scientific" credibility.**
   - The NASA 1975 Summer Study produced the Stanford torus (about 1.8 km ring,
     10,000 people, 1 g spin)
     ([Stanford torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_torus)). O'Neill's
     Island Three is a pair of counter-rotating 6.4–8 km-diameter cylinders
     ([O'Neill cylinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O'Neill_cylinder)).
   - NASA's HAVOC studied crewed airships about 50 km above Venus, where pressure
     is about 1 atm and the temperature about 75 °C
     ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/general/havoc/);
     [Space.com](https://www.space.com/29140-venus-airship-cloud-cities-incredible-technology.html)).
   - Shimizu's Ocean Spiral is a 500 m sphere for 5,000 people plus a helix to a
     seafloor base about 4 km down, powered by OTEC
     ([Shimizu](https://www.shimz.co.jp/en/topics/dream/content01/);
     [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2014/11/26/ocean-spiral-underwater-cities-shimizu-corporation/)).
   - Archigram's Walking City (Ron Herron, 1964) is the ancestor of every
     walking-city idea ([MoMA](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/813)).
5. **Sci-fi games keep space colonies and city-builders in the culture.**
   - 2026 has several sci-fi city-builders, for example *Beyond These Stars*
     (planned 1.0 in 2026) and a lunar-mining colony sim
     ([Gamestic](https://www.gamestic.nl/2026/03/09/sci-fi-city-builder-beyond-these-stars-skips-early-access-with-full-1-0-launch-planned-for-2026/);
     [Magic Game World](https://www.magicgameworld.com/top-10-upcoming-city-builders-and-management-sims-coming-in-2026/)).
   - *Surviving Mars* was relaunched in November 2025
     ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surviving_Mars)). This supports

### 2.1 Best-performing long-form styles (synthesised patterns [U])

1. **"How X could actually work"**: one concept, built system by system (structure →
   energy → water → food → transport → failure modes). This is our flagship
   series.
2. **Citizen's Walk**: a first-person, human-scale tour through a day in the city.
   It gives the highest immersion and watch time.
3. **Build It From Zero**: a construction timelapse from an empty site to a living
   city. This is the most satisfying structure and harvests easily into Shorts.
4. **Physics Check**: sci-fi tropes tested with a verdict. It drives comments.
5. **Scale journeys**: human → building → city → planet zoom-outs.

**Long-form beat sheet (8–12 min):**

| Time | Beat | Device |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Visual paradox + verbatim hook line | Hero clip, no logo |
| 5–10 s | Ident (5 s, pre-rendered) | §6.1 |
| 10–45 s | Promise: "By the end you'll know exactly how it stays up / powers itself / survives" | Schematic flash-forward |
| Every 60–90 s | "How it works" overlay beat | §4.4 |
| Every 90–120 s | Pattern interrupt: scale ghost, hard cut, or palette flip | §4.5 |
| Last 60–90 s | Plausibility Meter verdict + "what would break first" | §4.4 |

### 2.2 Best short-form styles

- **Scale Shock** (45–60 s): the object next to a known landmark, then a zoom-out.
- **60-Second City**: one mechanism, one reveal, one number.
- **Would You Live Here?**: a POV room or balcony tour ending on a question. The
  question drives comments.
- **Physics Check Shorts**: a trope, the verdict and the one number that decides
  it.
- **Hook rules:** motion in frame 1, on-screen hook text in the first 0.5 s, and
  the payoff before 70% of runtime. End on a loop (last frame matches the first)
  or a question.

### 2.3 Length sweet spots

- **Long-form:** 8–12 min is the core; flagship episodes run 12–15 min. At 8 min or
  more, mid-rolls are allowed.
- **Shorts:** 45–75 s for Scale Shock, 90–180 s for mini-explainers. YouTube Shorts
  allow **up to 3 min** for uploads since 15 Oct 2024
  ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)).
  Anything longer is TikTok/Reels only, with a ≤180 s YouTube cut.

## 4. Unified style bible

### 4.1 The look

- **"Speculative architectural cinema":** photoreal and physically plausible
  materials (glass, titanium, basalt-sintered regolith, CLT timber, bio-concrete,
  sapphire-like crystal, living green walls).
- **Engineering you can read:** visible structure (trusses, tethers, pressure
  hulls, spin rings), volumetric atmosphere and real scale cues (people, trees,
  trams).
- **Optimism by default (solarpunk):** greenery, clean air and warm light.
  **Cyberpunk** is used only for density or night episodes, as a deliberate
  contrast.
- **Every city has a logic.** Its form follows its physics: spheres under
  pressure, rings for spin gravity, hexagons for floating tessellation, legs
  spread to lower ground pressure.
- **Tone:** wonder with rigour. The camera marvels; the narration explains.

### 4.4 Signature devices (Remotion unless noted)

1. **Schematic → Living City reveal (the brand device).**
   - Clip A is a Deep Void schematic: cyan wireframe and cutaway lines on the
     same camera move.
   - Clip B is the photoreal, inhabited city (lights on, trams moving, birds or
     fish).
   - A 12-frame cyan scan-line wipe joins them. Use it in the cold open and once
     per chapter at most.
   - Build clip B with **Seedance 2.5 image-to-video**: `image_url` = a restyled
     last frame of clip A, so the geometry matches [U: test].
2. **"How It Works" physics overlay.**
   - A vector callout layer over footage: force arrows (gravity, buoyancy,
     tension, spin), energy-flow traces (animated dashed gold lines), and a mono
     label with one number ("Buoyancy 1.03 × weight", "Spin 1.9 rpm → 1 g").
   - Maximum 3 callouts on screen at once. Callouts draw on over 10 frames and
     hold at least 3 s.
3. **Plausibility Meter.** A 4-segment horizontal bar (PROVEN / STRETCH / FRONTIER
   / FICTION) with a needle. It appears for each subsystem and for the final
   verdict.
4. **Scale Ghost.** A translucent cyan silhouette of a known landmark: Burj Khalifa
   828 m, Eiffel 330 m, Empire State 381 m roof, football pitch 105 m, Manhattan
   outline, or a jumbo jet 70 m.
5. **Speculative Year Tag.** A top-right mono tag, e.g. `YEAR 2180 · SPECULATIVE`,
   plus the world name. It is always on during city beats (see §9).
6. **Atlas Map.** A stylised globe or planet with a pin that flies in to the city
   site (Remotion SVG, never AI). It reinforces the recurring world.
7. **Cutaway Slice.** A cross-section wipe that reveals interior layers
   (residential → farms → utilities → foundations), timed to the narration.

### 4.5 Camera grammar per beat type

| Beat type | Shot | Lens feel | Move | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Establishing / reveal** | Extreme wide aerial | 24 mm anamorphic | Push through cloud, water or rock, then crane up and pull back | Horizon in the top third; once per section at most |
| **Scale** | Wide, slow lateral | 35 mm | Lateral truck with a foreground human or vehicle | A known-size object is always in frame |
| **How it works** | Medium / cutaway | 50 mm clean-sharp | Slow push-in, or a locked shot with a slow orbit of 10–20° | Leave empty space for overlays (left or right third) |
| **Human scale (Citizen's Walk)** | Eye-level medium wide | 35 mm | Gimbal walk forward at walking speed | Figures seen from behind or at a distance; no identifiable faces |
| **Machine / vehicle** | Low tracking | 35–50 mm | Parallel track, or a chase from behind and to the side | Motion blur only on the background |
| **Build timelapse** | Wide, locked | 35 mm | Locked tripod; only time changes | The same camera text in every segment |
| **Space / orbit** | Extreme wide | 24 mm clean-sharp | Slow orbital arc, drift | Hard single-source sunlight; black sky, no stars when the sun is in frame |
| **Underwater** | Wide to medium | 24–35 mm | Slow glide forward, rising bubbles | Light falls off with depth; blue-green shift below 30 m |
| **Physics Check verdict** | Medium, locked | 50 mm | Very slow push | Calm; the meter lands |

**Rules:**

- Move on one axis at a time, and let a move breathe for at least 4 s.
- No handheld shake, except in construction or earthquake beats.
- No whip pans and no Dutch angles unless the beat is a failure or collapse.

### 4.6 Lighting rules

- **Default:** golden hour to blue hour, with a warm key, cool fill and lit
  windows.
- **Solarpunk day:** the morning after rain: wet reflective surfaces, soft sun and
  greenery.
- **Cyberpunk:** night rain with neon. It is only for megacity-density episodes.
- **Space:** a single hard sun, deep shadows and no atmospheric haze. Earthshine
  and Moonshine are allowed.
- **Underground:** practical light sources (crystal glow, light pipes, sun-shafts
  through mirrors). Crystals glow a cool cyan-violet, never rainbow.
- **Underwater:** caustics near the surface; darkness plus bioluminescent accents
  at depth.

### 4.8 Do / Don't

**Do:**

- Show structure, energy source and scale in every city.
- Show weather and atmosphere, and plausible materials.
- Show the physics as an overlay.
- Label speculation.
- Keep one recurring world per concept.
- Let the camera breathe.

**Don't:**

- Use magic tech with no explanation, or rainbow "energy crystals".
- Put flying cars in every shot.
- Put hologram ads everywhere (except in deliberate cyberpunk beats).
- Show identifiable faces, real people, real logos or franchise-specific designs
  (no Star Wars or Marvel lookalikes).
- Generate text inside the AI video (all text is added in Remotion).
- Present a speculative city as a real place or real footage.

### 5.4 When to use Seedance 2.5

1. **Clip B of the Schematic → Living reveal:** `image_url` = the restyled last
   frame of clip A, so the geometry lines up.
2. **Before/after and Build It From Zero stages:** `image_url` = the stage N still
   and `end_image_url` = the stage N+1 still, when an exact start and end state is
   needed.
3. **Continuity repair:** when a Cinema segment drifts at a chained boundary,
   regenerate that segment with Seedance using the previous last frame as
   `image_url`.
4. **Vertical Short loops:** set `end_image_url` = the first frame of the Short,
   which gives a perfect loop.

Use Cinema Studio 4.0 for everything else. It controls genre, lens and palette
better and keeps the house look.

### 5.5 Continuity across stitched segments

**App rules.**

- Each section is its own clip.
- A section longer than 30 s is split into `ceil(sec/30)` generations, each at
  least 4 s long.
- Generations are chained: the last frame of segment N becomes the keyframe of
  N+1.

**Channel split convention.** Use greedy 30 s chunks with the remainder last
(75 → 30+30+15). Never leave a 1–3 s remainder. Rebalance it instead
(62 → 30+28+4).

1. **One continuous move per section.** Every segment prompt repeats the same
   move, direction, speed and height band. Nothing starts or stops at a boundary.
2. **Section lock.** A `section_lock` string prepended to every segment fixes the
   time of day, weather, palette, lens, camera model, era, world and materials.
3. **Progressive framing.** Each segment says where the camera *starts*
   ("continuing from mid-altitude, facing the breakwater") and where it drifts.
4. **Continuous secondary motion.** Trams keep gliding and water keeps rolling
   across the boundary. Don't start an action in N that resolves in N+1 (doors,
   launches, a character entering).
5. **Boundary hygiene.** In the last 1 s of a segment, avoid particles,
   explosions, near-camera objects and fast pans. Motion-blurred keyframes
   degrade N+1.
6. **Deliberate hard cuts only at section boundaries**, on narration beats: a
   new system, a number reveal, a palette flip, or the verdict. Remotion handles
   the transition (hard cut, scan-line wipe, or a 6–8 frame dip to Deep Void).
7. **Series continuity.** Pass atlas stills as `image_urls` in every episode that
   revisits a city.

### 5.6 Cost controls

- Draft at reduced resolution and generate the 720p final only after script
  approval.
- Reuse each atlas establishing clip up to 3 times across the channel, recoloured
  or re-timed.
- Put data-heavy beats over slow, reused background clips with Remotion overlays.
- **Budget:** long-form ≤ $150 (≈ 729 s generated); Shorts ≤ $40.

## 7. Narration

- **Persona: "The Cartographer."** An unseen guide who maps worlds that do not
  exist yet. Curious, precise and warm, with a wonder that is earned by
  explanation. Speaks like an engineer who also reads poetry.
- **Pace:**
  - 145–155 WPM baseline.
  - 125–135 WPM on reveals and verdicts.
  - About 165 WPM on "how it works" number runs.
  - 0.6–1.0 s pauses after the big number or reveal.
- **Language rules:**
  - Use conditional verbs for speculation ("would", "could", "in this design").
  - Say "today, we can already…" for real science.
  - Use one vivid sensory detail per section (sound, temperature, light).
  - Banned: "insane", "mind-blowing", "scientists are baffled".
- **ElevenLabs voice direction** (a designed or library voice, **never a clone
  of a real person**):

  > "Warm, clear mid-baritone narrator, 35–45, neutral transatlantic accent.
  > Calm wonder, as if describing a place he has personally walked through;
  > precise and unhurried on technical terms, a quiet smile on reveals; crisp
  > consonants on numbers; intimate, never breathy, never trailer-voice."

  Starting settings [U, tune by ear]:
  - stability 0.45–0.55
  - similarity 0.75
  - style 0.20
  - speaker boost on
- **Sample paragraph (40 words):**

  > "Forty thousand people live on this city, and every few minutes it takes a
  > step. Each leg lands on a pad wider than a tennis court, so the sand barely

## 9. Compliance

1. **Speculative framing must be explicit (channel rule).**
   - A persistent top-right **`YEAR 2xxx · SPECULATIVE`** tag appears on every
     speculative city or vehicle beat.
   - The first 30 s of narration contains a framing line, e.g. "This city doesn't
     exist, yet. Here's how it could."
   - The description opens with: *"Speculative design visualisation. The cities
     and machines shown are imagined; the physics references are real and linked
     below."*
   - Never use "footage of", "leaked", "real", "discovered" or "secret project"
     for imagined content. Titles must not imply existence.
   - Real science (Moon caves, DEEP habitats, the diamond battery, Oxford
     teleportation) is shown as a **stylised diagram or a labelled "concept
     visualisation"**, with a dated source line ("Source: Nature Astronomy,
     2024").
2. **AI disclosure guidance.**
   - YouTube requires disclosure when realistic content could be mistaken for a
     real person, place, scene or event. Clearly unrealistic, animated or
     special-effects content does not require it
     ([YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/)).
     Since May 2026 the AI label is displayed more prominently [U: secondary]
     ([minimatters](https://minimatters.com/youtube-altered-or-synthetic-content-disclosure/)).
   - **Channel policy: set "Altered or synthetic content" = Yes on every video.**
     Our renders are photoreal, and some are set in real places (Earth's oceans,
     the real Moon). Disclosing costs nothing and protects trust.
   - TikTok: turn on the AI-generated content label. Meta: turn on the "AI info"
     label.
   - The description also carries: "Visuals generated with AI video tools;
     narration by a synthetic voice; script and research by a human editor."
3. **Inauthentic content policy.** Since 15 Jul 2025, "repetitious content" was
   renamed "inauthentic content" and covers mass-produced, template-like videos
   ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en);
   [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/7/youtube-updates-its-policy-to-demonetize-inauthentic-mass-produced-ai-generated-content)).
   Our safeguards:
   - Every episode has a unique thesis, a written script with sourced science,
     a unique shot plan and a verdict.
   - Series share branding, not stories.
   - Log the human review gates (idea, script and fact-check, final cut) in the
     app.
   - No music-only compilations. The 30–60 min "atlas" compilations are allowed
     only as edited, narrated re-cuts with new framing [U: policy risk; keep
     them rare].
4. **Science accuracy.**
   - Each script carries a source table: claim, number, URL and access date.
   - Use primary sources (NASA NTRS, journals, university releases) where
     possible.
   - Tag every subsystem with a Plausibility tier.
   - Anything invented (e.g. an "energy crystal" beyond the diamond battery) is
     tagged FRONTIER or FICTION on screen and in narration.
   - Pin a corrections comment when needed.
5. **IP and likeness.**
   - No franchise designs (e.g. named sci-fi cities, ships or characters) and no
     prompts that name artists or studios.
   - Refer to franchises only editorially and in narration, without imitating
     the visuals.
   - No real-person likeness and no voice clones.
   - Real companies (Shimizu, DEEP, N-ARK, OCEANIX) are named editorially, with
     no logos.
6. **Sponsorships:** tick "includes paid promotion". Sponsors never change a
   Physics Check verdict.
