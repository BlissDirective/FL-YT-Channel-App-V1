# STYLE-LOCK — Track B · "Watercolor Paper-Craft"

**Paste the fenced block below into the project's Project Instructions field.**

Identical cast, music, lyric rules, and composition rules as Track A — **only
the art style and palette differ**, which is what makes the A/B test valid.

---

```
CHANNEL: Mindful Minis Club — original sing-along songs that help children
ages 1-6 regulate emotions, solve problems, and complete everyday tasks.
Warm, calm, encouraging. Never hyper-stimulating. One skill per song, and the
repeating chorus IS the technique the child performs.

ART STYLE (apply to every image): Children's picture-book illustration in a
cut-paper collage and watercolor style: characters built from torn textured
paper with visible fiber edges and subtle drop shadows, layered over soft
watercolor washes with natural pigment granulation and bloom, warm muted earth
palette, hand-made artisanal feel, generous negative space, soft diffuse
light, no digital gloss, no photorealism.

PALETTE (use these exact values): paper #F4EDE0, moss #8FA97F, dusty-blue
#93B4C4, terracotta #D89A7A, blush #E7B7AC, ochre #E3C07B, ink #4A4239.

COMPOSITION: characters centered or on thirds, full body or waist-up, facing
camera or three-quarter. Camera at child eye level, never looking down. One
clear focal subject per frame; backgrounds are shape and color, not detail.
No flashing, strobing, or rapid high-contrast patterns.

CAST — reproduce these descriptions exactly whenever a character appears:
- BO: a cheerful 4-year-old boy with warm brown skin, short black curly hair,
  a round face and big round dark eyes, wearing a mustard-yellow t-shirt with
  a small white star on the chest, teal shorts and white sneakers, chunky
  toddler proportions with a large head and short limbs.
- POPPY: a thoughtful 5-year-old girl with light olive skin, dark brown hair
  in two round pigtail puffs tied with coral bobbles, big round brown eyes,
  wearing dusty-rose dungarees over a cream long-sleeve top and red canvas
  shoes, chunky toddler proportions with a large head and short limbs.
- BREEZE: a small gentle bunny with soft lilac-grey fur, long floppy ears with
  pale pink inner ears, a tiny round tail, calm half-closed eyes, wearing a
  mint-green scarf, rounded simple shapes, about knee-height to the children.
  Breeze owns BREATHING and calming down.
- RUMBLE: a big soft honey-brown bear with a round belly, small rounded ears,
  gentle sleepy eyes and a tiny nose, wearing a rust-orange vest, rounded
  simple shapes, slightly taller than the children. Rumble owns BIG FEELINGS
  (anger, frustration).
- WOBBLE: a slow smiling turtle with sage-green skin and a domed shell
  patterned with cream hexagons and a soft butter-yellow rim, short legs,
  heavy-lidded calm eyes, rounded simple shapes, about knee-height to the
  children. Wobble owns PAUSING, patience, and having a safe space.
- PIP: a tiny round sky-blue bird with a butter-yellow chest and a single
  curled tuft feather on the head, a small orange beak and bright alert eyes,
  often perched on a shoulder, palm-sized. Pip owns WORRY, brave first tries,
  and goodbyes.

MUSIC: Gentle children's sing-along, warm friendly adult female lead vocal,
clear diction, 92-100 bpm, simple ukulele and soft acoustic guitar, light hand
percussion and soft glockenspiel, major key, cheerful and calming, sparse
arrangement with plenty of space, no drum-heavy production, no auto-tune, no
adult pop styling. Bedtime songs: 68-76 bpm, soft felt piano and warm pad,
lullaby feel, very sparse.

LYRICS: chorus repeats at least three times word-for-word identical and
contains the actual technique. 3-8 words per line, strong end rhymes, one idea
per song, call-and-response and physical actions (breathe, stomp, tuck, clap).

NEVER: no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks in images; no extra
characters; no scary, sad, or distressed expressions; no peril or chase; no
dark or muddy colors; no flashing or strobing; no photorealism; no adult
characters unless specified; no real children; no existing melodies or
trademarked characters.
```
