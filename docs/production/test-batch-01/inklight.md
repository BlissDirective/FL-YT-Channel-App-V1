### 0.3 Global QC gate (applies to every brief; per-video specifics are in each brief)
| Check | Reject if | Fix path |
|---|---|---|
| Character drift | Accent color shifts hue for > 6 frames; an accessory is missing or swapped (scarf, goggles/backpack, topknot/staff, visor, crown); a size swap (Pip ≥ Bolt's shoulder, Brick thinner than Bolt) | Re-roll the segment from the same keyframe; if it repeats, regenerate the keyframe |
| Extra limbs | A 3rd arm/leg, a fused limb, or a limb passing through the body visible ≥ 3 frames (1–2 frames may be hidden under an impact frame or cut) | Re-roll; shorten the action to "one clear action per shot" |
| Faces appearing | Any mouth, nose, teeth, brows, skin tone or human face, even for 1 frame (zero tolerance) | Re-roll; strengthen NEG; eyes-only close-ups |
| Style break | Flat/white/empty background, a 2D-cartoon look, a photoreal human, a lost rim light, blood instead of ink sparks | Re-roll; restate STYLE_LOCK in full |
| Stitch-seam continuity | At a 30s boundary: a position jump > 5% of frame width, a lighting-direction flip, a prop changing hands, a screen-direction reversal, a visible "reset" of motion | Regenerate only the later segment from the prior segment's last frame; or hide the seam with a whip-pan/impact frame in edit |
| Text artifacts | Any legible or pseudo-letter glyphs in the generated plate (signs, buttons, screens) | Mask/blur in Remotion, or re-roll with "blank unmarked surfaces"; all real text is composited in Remotion |

## 1. Viral format study (2024–2026)

### 1.1 What the evidence says
- **Alan Becker's escalating-rules episodes are long, wordless and still huge.** *Animation vs. Math* runs about 14 minutes with no dialogue. It escalates from simple numbers to reality-bending equations and passed **26M views in its first month** [R1]. The *Animation vs. Education* line continued with *Animation vs. Coding* (premiered 2025-03-01) [R3]. Becker's most-viewed video, *Cave Spider Roller Coaster (AvM Shorts Ep. 14)*, is an **8-minute** ride-gauntlet episode with about **522M views as of July 2026** [R2]. The channel reads about 34.1M subscribers [S1][R17].
- **Indie pilots are the biggest "first impression" in animation on YouTube.** *The Amazing Digital Circus* pilot (2023-10-13) passed **400M views** and is described as the most-viewed indie animation pilot on YouTube [R4]. The *Murder Drones* pilot has **83M+ (May 2026)** and is the series' most-viewed episode. It is built on a cold open, a narrated premise, A/B plots mixing threat with comedy, and a cliffhanger [R5]. *Takeaway: episode 1 of a new world outperforms later episodes, so the pilot is the flagship asset.*
- **Micro-drama Parts follow a 3-act structure inside 60–90s.** The hook lands in the first ~3s (conflict or shock by ~8s), the escalation of ~40–50s stacks 2–3 tension beats, and a cliffhanger ends every episode [R6].
- **Shorts mechanics.**
  - **Swipe decision:** the "viewed vs. swiped away" ratio is decided in the first 1–3s. 50–60% of drop-offs happen in the first 3s, and a 2026 benchmark is **≥ 80% held at 3s, ≥ 60% at midpoint, ≥ 70% APV** [R8][R16] [U: third-party benchmarks].
  - **Pacing:** change something every 1–2s and open a micro-loop every 5–8s [R8].
  - **Loops:** a verbal callback plus a visual match cut is the strongest loop device, and loops push APV above 100% [R7].
  - **View counting:** since March 2025, loops count as views, and "engaged views" exclude them [R8b].
  - **Length:** Shorts can be up to 3 min (since 2024-10-15). Sub-60s Shorts sit in a faster feed [R9], and 20–45s performs best for pure loops [R9b] [U].
- **AI-native animation has two proven poles.**
  - **Absurdist slop:** *Italian brainrot* is loud, fast and short, with one Short at about 320M views, and it pushes completion past 70% [R12]. That same slop profile is what YouTube's 2025–26 inauthentic-content enforcement targets [S18][S20].
  - **Authored lore:** *Neural Viz* built a persistent universe with hand-cut timing and continuity, and gets hundreds of thousands to millions of views per episode [R13].
  - **Seedance hype:** Seedance 2.0 (Feb 2026) went viral mainly through real-actor and IP deepfakes [R14], which we must never emulate (Bible §9.1).
  - *Takeaway: borrow the brainrot pacing, not the brainrot content. Pair it with Neural-Viz-grade authorship.*
- **Stick-fight Shorts:** "legendary" stick fights (e.g., XiaoXiao-lineage fights re-uploaded as Shorts [R11]) and automated procedural stick-fight pipelines [R11b] show sustained demand for pure choreography. Exact view counts are [U].
- **Thumbnails:** use 1–3 words (< 12 characters), one high-contrast subject about 30% brighter or darker than the background, and 2–3 bold colors. Faceless channels substitute a clear, emotive subject for a human face [R15].

### 1.3 INKLIGHT individualization layer (applied to every format)
1. **Look:** ink-and-light only. Glossy black tube bodies with emissive rims in hyper-real lit worlds, and tiny heroes against huge sets (scale is the thumbnail). Rim brightness is an **emotion meter**: dim = hurt or scared, flare = inspired. Every fight and gag must state its rim cue.
2. **Recurring cast:** each format is "owned" by a lead so the batch also tests characters. **F3 pilot = ensemble/Bolt · F2 action = Nova · F1-style escalation comedy = Pip · heart/horror-lite = Brick.** A name tag (Inter SemiBold, accent color) appears at each character's first appearance per video.
3. **Lore:** every video drops exactly one lore beat (the First Draft, the Nib shards, Smudge watching) but stays watchable standalone. Test-batch rule: **no episode numbers on screen** (the batch skips E02/E05). The canon order is fixed after the test.
4. **Sound identity:**
   - **Signature "drip-tink"** (an ink drip plus a glass tink) on the ident, on every Nib or shard appearance, and as the Shorts' first sound.
   - **Leitmotifs:** Bolt = rising brass 4-note, Pip = marimba, Nova = koto-synth, Brick = tuba, Smudge = detuned music box.
   - **Hits** are glassy tinks, never flesh.
5. **Editing signature:**
   - **Ink-bloom wipe** on hard cuts between locations: an ink drop spreads and reveals the next shot (≤ 12 frames).
   - **Rim-colored impact frames** (≤ 3 per fight).
   - **Record-scratch freeze-frame** with the character's name card for comedy.
   - **"Rim flare = music hit"** sync: every flare lands on a downbeat.
   - **Hard cut to black** on cliffhangers, then the drip-tink.
