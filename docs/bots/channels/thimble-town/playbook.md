# Thimble Town: Channel Playbook

**Channel:** Thimble Town (franchise umbrella: *Tiny Worlds*). Tagline: *"Life in the tiny worlds."*
**What it is:** cozy miniature diorama worlds told through gentle "life in the tiny world" stories. A mouse runs a ramen stand, a snail lights the lamps, a wren carries the post. Tilt-shift macro look, warm palette, ASMR foley. The main track is wordless.
**Default model:** Seedance 2.5 i2v (`hf-seedance-2-5`) for every story section. Cinema Studio 4.0 (`hf-cinema-studio-4`) only for establishing wides at hard cuts.
**Voices today:** none. Residents never speak. A narrator ("The Keeper of the Lamps") exists only on paper until the director approves a voice.

This playbook holds the channel-specific knowledge. The shared operating rules win on any conflict.

---

## 1. Channel identity

**Promise to the viewer:** a place you visit and residents you know. Every upload is one small story in one tiny world (a rainy night, a build, a round of the lamps, a festival), with a beginning, a small event per section, and a lights-on or lights-out ending. The residents cook and build; no human does.

**Audience:** general and adult cozy viewers (study, sleep, ASMR, lo-fi, cozy-games fans), targeting 18–44, global (wordless = no localisation). **Not made for kids** at channel level.

**Tone:** calm, warm, unhurried, satisfying. Stillness sells the miniature. Emotional beats are small (a pressed leaf, a wave, a nod).

**The Thimble Town signature (every video):**
- **Residents** Miso, Bramble, Lumen and Wren with cast blocks pasted verbatim; walk-ons from the locked list.
- **Lore with addresses:** Noodle Row, Lantern Lane, the Spool Bridge, the Teacup Harbour, the Thimble Tower. **Builds persist:** the teapot bakery and Lumen's matchbox shed are canon sets in later episodes. Wren's pressed-leaf letter points ahead to *Bottlecap City*.
- **Real-calendar season.** Autumn runs September–November (leaf drifts, acorn caps, pumpkins; `oil-ochre` and `twilight-fable`). Winter runs December–February, spring March–May, summer June–August.
- **Texture:** needle-felt fibres, resin sheen and painted-wood brush marks in every keyframe, and **one everyday object as architecture in every shot** (the scale cue and visual trademark).
- **Palette** 70/30 warm/cool; Lantern Amber in every night frame; nothing pure black.
- **Foley signature:** the brand lamp *tink* (identical everywhere), rain-on-tin, ladle on ceramic, and the music-box C–E–G motif at each episode's emotional payoff.

**What we never do:**
- Photoreal animals, human faces, human hands or fingers (no "giant hand" moments; paws are felted/resin, never five-fingered). If a human hand ever appears, the AI disclosure goes on.
- Characters talking, lip-syncing or mouths moving as if talking.
- Text in pictures; on-screen text in long-form (chapters live in the description); lettering or signage anywhere.
- Fast cuts, whip pans, handheld shake, fast zooms, Dutch angles.
- Peril, injury, scary storms, spreading fire (fire is only a contained stove, tealight or candle glow).
- Neon, saturated primaries, pure black or white, cold palettes without an amber anchor; brand logos (except sponsor props).
- The same set + weather + activity in two consecutive uploads; templated AI "tiny workers" or "glass fruit" loops without a story.
- Titles with "I built…", "handmade", "kids", "toddlers" or "children"; nursery rhymes, counting or ABCs.

## 2. Look & sound bible

### 2.1 Locked look
- **Medium:** hand-built miniature diorama. Found-object architecture (teacup houses, a thimble water tower, a spool café table, matchbox sheds, a tin-can lighthouse). Surfaces: painted wood, felt, clay, paper, real micro-props.
- **Characters:** needle-felted and hand-painted resin figurines about 2–4 cm tall, visibly miniature craft.
- **Optics:** macro 60–100mm look, shallow DOF (f/2.8 equivalent), a horizontal tilt-shift focus band with strong falloff top and bottom, bokeh "fairy lights" from practicals, fine 35mm grain, vignette ≤15%.
- **Scale cues in every establishing shot:** visible felt fibres, brush marks, oversized dust motes in light shafts, a giant everyday object at frame edge (a pencil, a mug rim, a fern leaf).
- **Motion (after Keith Loutit):** residents move slightly toy-like (small steps, gentle bobbing); steam and water move at real speed. That contrast sells the miniature.
- **Lighting:** golden hour default for exteriors (low 15–25° key from camera-left, Honey); practicals tell the story (tiny windows, string lights, stove glow, lantern posts); **blue hour + amber windows is the money shot** and the ident moment; low-density haze allowed.

**Landing still (`endFrame {prompt}`):** the same keyframe prompt with only the pose sentence replaced by the intended end pose. Cast and set blocks stay verbatim.

### 2.7 Motion prompt template (Seedance `videoPrompt`)
```
Continuous single shot inside a miniature diorama, tilt-shift macro, shallow depth
of field. Camera: {very slow push-in | slow lateral macro dolly left-to-right | locked top-down | slow pull-back | slow crane up}, constant speed, no cuts. {RESIDENT} {one clear action, verb + object}, small toy-like movements.
Ambient motion: {steam curling | rain drips | lamp flicker | leaves drifting}. Lighting stays {LIGHT STATE}. Warm cozy palette, fine film grain.
```
For a section >30s, give each segment its own prompt through `segments` (for example, the brief's seg 1 / seg 2). Sections >30s must use `segments`: a list of {sec 4–30, prompt} that adds up to the section's seconds. Each segment gets its own verbatim prompt and starts from the previous segment's last frame (Cinema Studio can't truly continue a frame, so its segment prompts must each stand alone). Put every segment boundary on a natural motion beat. `estimate_directed` warns about any >30s section without segments. Landed segments are saved, so a retry never pays for them twice.

### 2.8 Model choice per shot type
| Need | Model | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Every story section (any resident) | Seedance 2.5 i2v | `keyframePrompt` always (never text-to-video a resident); `endFrame {prompt}` landing pose on each section; `generateAudio: true` for sync-critical foley |
| Establishing wides, weather sweeps, town crane-ups, "other world" arrivals, ident/outro plates | Cinema Studio 4.0 | **Only at hard cuts** (it cannot continue a chain or take `endFrame`). `controls`: `genre drama`, `pacing calm` (or `single-shot` for crane-ups), `camera_model 35mm-film`, `camera_lens warm-vintage` (`halation-vintage` at night), `color_palette` by mood; leave `era` unset. 1–3 SOUL set/cast refs in `refs` |
| Abstract inserts with no cast (rain on tin, steam) | Seedance 2.5 (no keyframe) | Cheap filler |

Cinema palettes by mood: `twilight-fable` dusk/night · `the-morning-after-rain` rain and fog mornings · `yellow-room` interiors · `breakfast-on-schedule` morning openings · `oil-ochre` autumn · `a-dream-in-color` spring/summer · `the-faded-fresco` memory/flashback · `pink-velvet` winter-holiday dusk only. Never `horror` or `action`.

Cinema establishing prompt form: `Establishing shot of {WORLD}, a hand-built miniature town made of {FOUND OBJECTS}, tilt-shift macro look, {TIME/WEATHER}, tiny amber windows, {slow crane up over rooftops | slow orbit}, gentle toy-like motion of tiny figures in the distance, no people, no text.`

### 2.9 Audio
- **Native audio (`generateAudio: true`)** for on-screen, sync-critical foley: ladle clink, sizzle, knife taps, door bell, footsteps on wood, rain on *that* awning. The pipeline keeps clip audio in the mix, so if a clip's audio carries music, voices or mumbling, re-roll it or set `generateAudio: false`.
- **Signature sounds as SFX cues with fixed prompt strings** (identical every time): brand tink `"a single soft bright brass lamp tink, a tiny glass lamp head lighting up, clean short chime with a gentle shimmer decay, no music, no voices"`; rain bed `"gentle steady rain pattering on a small tin roof and awning, soft drips, cozy, close-mic, no thunder, no voices, no music"`; night bed `"soft distant crickets chirping on a calm autumn night, gentle seamless ambience, no voices, no music"`. Weather beds run under the whole video, one cue per section; SA split a 27s section's bed into 22s + 5s cues.
- **Music:** lo-fi (70–80 BPM, dusty keys, vinyl crackle), music box/celesta (C–E–G motif), fingerpicked nylon guitar, soft felt piano. Seasonal colour: autumn cello pads, winter sleigh-bell whisper and glockenspiel, summer kalimba and cicadas, spring flute. Music always sits under the foley and **never plays in the first 5s**. SA: `"soft lo-fi electric piano keys, warm and cozy, slow tempo around 70 bpm, gentle and sparse, rainy night ramen shop mood, instrumental, no vocals, no drums for the first 5 seconds"`, `gainDb -20`, `underVoDb 0`.
- **Mix targets:** foley −18 to −14 LUFS short-term, music −26 to −22, narration −16, master −14 LUFS integrated.
- **Foley library to draw from:** ladle on ceramic, broth simmer, noodle swish, chopstick taps, tea pour, oven door creak, flour puff, rolling pin, shop door bell, paper rustle, envelope seal, pencil scratch, clock tick, rain on tin, drips into a thimble, distant thunder, wind through leaves, snow crunch, footsteps on wood and cobble, lantern squeak, frogs, fireflies (soft shimmer), tiny train clack, boat creak, water lapping, wood-block taps, sanding, brush strokes, glue click, tiny hammer.

### 2.10 Labels, captions, sting, ident, outro, watermark
- **Captions:** `captions: false` on every video (wordless). Upload an English CC track of sound descriptions (`[rain on a tin roof]`, `[broth simmering]`).
- **Long-form:** no on-screen text. Chapters are storybook-named in the description.
- **Shorts text policy (under test):** none (SA, SC) · one 2s label (SB: `tiny pumpkin`, top, `title`, `#F6EAD3`) · 1–2-word stage labels for builds (SD: `matchbox`, `roof`, `door`, `Lumen's shed`; top, `label`, `#F6EAD3`, ~1.5–2s at each stage's start). Lower-case, cream.
- **Shorts:** frame 1 = hook (extreme macro of a glowing, steaming or moving thing); scale reveal by 5–8s; 0.5s `sting` right after the reveal (SA `at 6.2`, SB `5.2`, SD `3.5`); watermark on; loop with `endFrame {fromSection: 0}` and `outro {mode: "overlay", sec: 2.5, cta: "Full episode: <long-form title>"}`. Keep action out of the top 12% and bottom 20% of the 9:16 frame (UI), with the tilt-shift band at ~55% height.
- **Long-form:** cold open (5–6s macro hook, no music, no text) → **ident "Lamps On" (5s) at ~0:05–0:11**, never at 0:00 → story sections → **outro 18s**. Ident: blue-hour wide (Cinema, `twilight-fable`, `single-shot`): Lumen's lantern touches the first lamp (*tink*, amber), the glow cascades down Lantern Lane window by window, the thimble tower catches the last glow; the wordmark is a label; audio = music-box C–E–G (celesta), lamp tink, distant crickets. Outro: slow pull-back from the final lit window to a town wide, then lights dimming one by one with space for two end-screen slots and a subscribe circle over the thimble tower; 12s music-box reprise, then rain or crickets. Generate both once and `reuse` them.
- **Intro sting (1.5s):** the motif's last note and one window lighting up, used as a chapter transition before at most two sections per long-form (the script holds one `sting`, so pick the most important).
- **Joins:** hard cuts (`cut`) at section boundaries, match cuts where the end pose equals the next keyframe; for time jumps the brief uses a 12-frame dip-to-warm, and the closest directed transition is `dipToBlack`.

## 4. Formats that work

Each card: the reference pattern → the Thimble Town twist.

| Format | Reference evidence | Hook | Pacing / payoff / loop | Our twist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **F1 Real tiny cooking** | Miniature Space, Miniature Cusina, Tastemade *Tiny Kitchen* (600M+ views in ~18 months) | A famous dish at impossible scale | Calm wordless process; dish reveal at ~80–90% | A resident cooks, not a hand. Cooking paired with weather and customers: the dish is the midpoint payoff, the last customer is the ending. Found-object kitchen; amber vs rain slate; rain-on-tin + ladle on ceramic |
| **F2 Impossible-material cut ASMR** | AI glass-fruit ASMR (#AIASMR ~640M views in 90 days [U]) | Blade already touching the object in frame 1 | One cut, one crisp transient; loops on matching silent frames | The mechanic, not the material: blade contact in frame 1 + dry hero transient + a scale reveal, applied to a story craft (Bramble carving a pea-sized pumpkin) |
| **F3 Mini world / tiny workers** | AI tilt-shift Shorts, 15–60s | Giant everyday object with tiny workers mid-task | 5–10s segments, slow drift; task complete by the end | Named residents and one locked crew (Builder Beetles); addresses; craft texture; every build becomes canon |
| **F4 Cozy lo-fi ambience** | Lofi Girl (~15.85M), Cozy Rain | An instantly legible mood | Near-static micro-motion; continuous bed; seamless loop | Rain-on-tin under the whole episode; every episode ends on lights on/out; sections feed Night Lights sleep cuts at no new cost |

| **F7 Build / transformation** | Nerdforge-style builds, diorama build Shorts | Flash of the finished result (~0.3–1s), then the raw object | Stage by stage, each ending on a visible completion; "lights on" at ~75% | The residents build (never "I built"); the build has a canon reason and becomes a permanent set |

### Long-form (16:9, 6–12 min; batch 7:29–9:00)
One place, one time arc (dusk→night, build start→opening night). **6–12 calm sections**, each with **one small event** landing in its middle third (a customer arrives, rain starts, a lamp is lit). Reveal payoff at 70–80%. Long holds, very slow moves, no text, a soft bed under detailed foley, storybook chapters ("4:10 The first customer"). First 30s: macro cold open (pattern interrupt) → ident → an establishing shot that promises the arc. Rotate series: Shop Diaries → Build a Tiny → Tiny Seasons (calendar-matched) → Tiny Travels → Night Lights.

### Shorts (9:16 native, 60–180s)
One satisfying process or one micro-story, built as **4–6 self-contained micro-payoff beats of 10–27s**. Each beat opens on a fresh satisfying event, with small events (a placement, a tap, a tink) every 3–7s inside it: the cozy-speed version of the 1.5–2s stimulus rule. Never rely on one continuous 60s+ action. Scale reveal by 5–8s; payoff by ~70%; composition-matched last frame for the loop. Write the ending first. Proven: **SA "One Tiny Bowl of Ramen in the Rain"** (92s, 5 Seedance sections, no text).

**Titles:** `[cozy moment/time/weather] + at/in + [the Tiny ___ / named place]`, ≤60 chars, front-load "Tiny" or "Miniature"; optional `| Thimble Town`. Build titles: `Building a Tiny ___ Inside a ___` or `[Object] → [Place]`. Thumbnails: one extreme-macro hero object filling 50–60%, the resident small for scale, the warm glow brightest, dark plum/night surround, 0–3 words in cream rounded serif, series tick top-left.

## 5. Script-writing recipe

1. **Pick the idea** from the queue; check season, series rotation and the distinctness rule (not the same set + weather + activity as the previous upload). Pick one weather state: clear golden, soft rain, fog morning, first snow, windy leaf-fall, distant thunderstorm, heat-haze summer night, starry clear.
2. **Write the ending first** (loop frame for Shorts; lights on/out for long-form), then the hook (extreme macro in frame 1).
3. **Beat/section plan.** Shorts: 5 sections of 10–27s (SA: 12, 23, 27, 18, 12). Long-form: 10–12 sections of 20–60s; the cold open 5–6s (Seedance), establishing sections 20–30s (Cinema, hard cuts only). Keep Short sections ≤30s. Long-form sections >30s use `segments` (for example 20+20, 30+30), one prompt per segment.
4. **keyframePrompt** from §2.6 with the verbatim set block and resident block(s), one frozen pose sentence, lighting, weather and season lines, the negative line and the aspect. One resident action per shot. Fewer figurines per frame = less drift (five in one frame was LF1's highest-risk section).
5. **endFrame:** `{prompt}` = the keyframe prompt with the end-pose sentence swapped in (every section); last Short section `{fromSection: 0}`.
6. **videoPrompt** from §2.7: the same camera verb as the keyframe's composition; one verb + object; ambient motion; light state.
7. **model** `hf-seedance-2-5` + `generateAudio: true` on story sections; Cinema sections carry `controls` + `refs` and no end frame.
8. **SFX:** a weather/room bed in every section; the hero transient of the beat (plink, crunch, nori crackle); the brand tink wherever a lamp lights.
9. **music** per §2.9 (no music in the first 5s; say so in the prompt). **labels** only per the Shorts text policy. **sting / outro / watermark / captions** per §2.10. **qc**: §6 shared criteria + this video's scale and loop checks. Metadata: title, 2 alt titles, description with "Stories and worlds created with AI tools.", tags.
