# Gaming-Compliant Source Lanes (GTA 6 / IP-heavy niches)

How the studio sources footage for gaming channels **without** infringing
publisher IP. This is the operating policy + the first-pass tooling. It extends
the Phase 6.5 Source Library, which deliberately excludes gaming gameplay from
the autonomous licensed-source agent.

> **Core rule:** the autonomous agent never touches publisher IP. Branded game
> footage enters **only** through a human-gated lane where the operator vouches
> for the rights. There is no "scrape YouTube / rip gameplay" path, ever.

---

## The four lanes

### 1. ✅ Autonomous licensed lane (already built)
The standard Source Library: Pexels, Pixabay, Openverse, Wikimedia — CC0 / CC-BY
/ Pexels / Pixabay only, deterministically licence-screened. **For gaming this
returns generic b-roll** (a controller, a neon arcade, a city skyline for a GTA
mood shot) — never the game itself. Great for atmosphere and cutaways.

### 2. ✅ Manual press-kit / own-capture lane (new — first pass)
At the Assets gate, the Source Library now has **"Add official press-kit /
own-capture asset"**: paste a URL to a publisher-permitted press asset or your
own recording, add a title + source, choose image/video, attach it to the shot.
- **Manually gated** — never reachable by the agent.
- **Attribution required** — a credit line is auto-added to the description.
- The operator vouches for the rights when attaching.

### 3. ⚠️ Official embed / reference lane (use, don't redistribute)
Official trailers and clips can be **referenced and linked**, and embedded on a
watch page — but **embedding ≠ downloading and re-uploading**. Use official
trailers for research/Scout and to *link* in descriptions; do not cut publisher
trailer footage into your render. Commentary/criticism may qualify as fair use,
but that's a per-video legal judgment the operator makes, not the app.

### 4. ❌ Forbidden
- Ripped gameplay, leaked footage, datamined assets, third-party streams/VODs.
- Re-uploading publisher trailers or cinematics into your render.
- Anything the licence can't be verified for. (The auto-lane rejects unknown
  licences by default; the manual lane relies on operator diligence.)

## Compliant source shortlist for GTA 6

| Source | What for | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Rockstar / Take-Two press kit & Newswire** | Official screenshots, key art, logos, fact-sheet imagery | Press/editorial use per the publisher's terms — read them per asset |
| **Own capture** | Your own recordings (once the game ships) / your own commentary B-roll | You own it |
| **Pexels / Pixabay** | Gaming-*adjacent* b-roll: controllers, arcades, city/neon moods, setups | Pexels/Pixabay licence (free, no attribution) |
| **Openverse / Wikimedia (CC)** | Maps, real-world Miami/“Vice City” reference imagery, public-domain stills | CC0 / CC-BY (credit auto-added) |
| **NASA / gov archives** | Establishing/landscape/aerial stock for mood | Public domain |

For factual/lore explainers, most shots can be carried by lane 1 + lane 4
(maps, real-world references, atmosphere), with a few **press-kit key-art**
stills (lane 2) for the recognizable GTA 6 visuals.

---

## Recommended GTA 6 channel workflow

1. **Format toward news/lore/analysis**, not gameplay showcases — these are
   carried by stills, key art, maps, and atmosphere, which the compliant lanes
   cover. (Gameplay-showcase channels need a licence the app can't grant.)
2. At the **Assets gate**, let the autonomous lane fill mood/cutaway beats, then
   attach **official press-kit key art** to the hero/identity beats via the
   manual lane.
3. Keep the **attribution ledger** clean — it ships every credit into the
   Publish Kit description automatically.
4. When in doubt about a clip's rights, **don't attach it.** A generic
   compliant b-roll shot is always safer than a borrowed one.
